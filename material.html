What Is MarmoFiber?

MarmoFiber is an engineered architectural material that combines marble-inspired mineral character with fiber reinforcement and controlled manufacturing.

01 — Material Overview

The emotional value of stone, with room to be engineered.

It is developed for architects and designers seeking the emotional value of stone together with greater flexibility in surface, texture, form, repetition, and application.

Where solid stone is quarried, cut and fixed within the limits of the block it came from, MarmoFiber is developed as an architectural system: a mineral surface layer with a reinforced body behind it, produced against a defined finish and a defined geometry.

That difference changes what a design team can ask of the surface — how it turns a corner, how it repeats across a façade, how a bespoke element is coordinated with the rest of the package.

“The character of marble. The intelligence of fiber.”

06 — Composition

Read as a build-up, not a slab.

Four engineered layers, from the protective coating on the face to the fiber cement that carries the load.

01

Protective coating

A polyurethane sealer applied over an epoxy sealer, specified per case of fixation and texture. It carries the UV resistance and the colour retention.

02

Marmo Fiber

The visible layer: natural materials, crushed marble, cement and selected aggregates, with latex, additives and synthetic iron oxide pigments.

03

Face mix / bonding coat

Silica flour, cement and latex — the bond between the Marmo Fiber layer and the GRC layer behind it.

04

Fiber cement layer

Approximately 50% cement and 50% silica with specialized additives, applied by spray-mix GRC machine. This is where the mechanical strength sits.

Diagram shown for design communication. Layer thicknesses, mix design and fixing methods are project-specific and confirmed through the latest approved MarmoFiber technical documents.

01 — Protective Coating

A specialized sealer coating, consisting of polyurethane applied over an epoxy sealer — a type of coating designed upon each case of fixation and texture — is used to preserve the surface appearance and enhance the long-term performance of the material. This coating provides UV resistance, colour retention and reduced water and vapour absorption, helping maintain MarmoFiber's original texture, finish and gloss over an extended period.

The coating can be applied at any stage following the initial installation and fixation, or during maintenance procedures, providing an additional layer of protection throughout the product's service life.

02 — Marmo Fiber

Marmo Fiber is a specially engineered admixture formulated according to the specific requirements of each type of MarmoFiber stone. It incorporates natural materials, crushed marble, cement and selected aggregates to achieve the required texture, dual-colour effect and surface pattern.

Latex and chemical additives are incorporated to enhance the bonding strength between the different layers. In addition, special synthetic iron oxides are used as pigments to provide stable coloration and minimize colour changes caused by prolonged exposure to UV radiation.

03 — Face Mix / Bonding Coat

The face mix, or bonding coat, is composed of silica flour, cement and latex, following a formulation similar to that used in GRC face mixes.

Its primary function is to create a strong, durable and homogeneous bond between the Marmo Fiber layer and the GRC layer, ensuring effective adhesion and long-term integration between the two materials.

04 — Fiber Cement Layer

The fiber cement layer is applied using a spray-mix GRC machine. The mix consists of approximately 50% cement and 50% silica, combined with specialized additives.

This composition is designed to provide the final product with the required mechanical strength and durability, while maintaining mechanical characteristics comparable to conventional GRC (glass reinforced concrete).

As a result, MarmoFiber combines the aesthetic qualities of natural stone with the structural performance and strength of GRC — a product that offers both visual appeal and long-term durability.

08 — Application-Based Engineering

The same finish, engineered differently for where it lands.

A finish specified on an external elevation and the same finish specified on an interior feature wall are not the same product decision. Exposure, substrate, fixing, panel size, joint strategy and site sequence all change.

MarmoFiber is developed application by application, so the appearance stays consistent while the build-up follows the requirement.
