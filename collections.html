01 — Five Collections

Five families. One material logic.

Each collection is a distinct architectural identity rather than a colour chart. Choose the family that matches the building's character, then narrow to the finish that behaves the way the elevation or the interior needs it to.

Marmo Vetrine

Open-pored, vein-cut surfaces with the linear movement of cut travertine, in nine tones from nude through to black.

Marmo Terra

Terrazzo-character surfaces: graded mineral chips set in a pale matrix and opened up by grinding.

Rough

The open textural family — a coarse broken grain in the rough finish, closer and shallower in the flamed.

Special Patterns

Relief surfaces with their own geometry: Linea fluting, the Carro grid, Marmo Vein ribbing and Marmo Stria coursing.

Special Design Features

Moulded architectural profiles — corniches, string courses and opening frames carried out in the same material.
